Frequently Asked Questions

Bonafide Certificate
Students should submit the prescribed request through the college office or student portal. The exact processing time and supporting documents depend on the institution.

Placement Support
Students should regularly check the official placement cell notices for company eligibility, registration deadlines, assessment links and interview schedules. Eligibility can differ from one company to another.

Scholarship
Students should check the official scholarship notice and submit documents before the announced deadline. Do not rely on unofficial social media messages for deadline confirmation.

Library
Students should carry their valid student identity card when borrowing books. Overdue rules and renewal limits are determined by the library.

Contact
For an official decision about academic eligibility, examination eligibility, certificates or university regulations, contact the concerned college office or department.
