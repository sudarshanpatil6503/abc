Attendance and Academic Regulations
Students should maintain the minimum attendance required by their institution to be eligible for university examinations. Attendance shortage, condonation and eligibility are subject to the applicable university regulations and college approval.

Examination Procedure
Students must carry their valid examination hall ticket or approved identity document. Students should reach the examination hall before the reporting time. Mobile phones and unauthorized electronic devices are not permitted inside the examination hall.

Leave and Examination Eligibility
A student who is absent because of approved leave should contact the department office and examination section as early as possible. Eligibility to appear for an examination depends on the applicable university regulations and the student's academic record.
